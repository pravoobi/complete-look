## Shot list by device

Record in two sessions, then cut the clips together in scene order.

**📱 Phone (screen recording, portrait): scenes 1–10, 12, 13.** This is most of the video. The rules ask to show the app running on its device, and this is a mobile-first shopping app.
- Before you start: screen recorder on, Do Not Disturb on, brightness up.
- **Clip A (scenes 1–9 and 13):** open https://complete-look.vercel.app and tap Ananya. Then do the scene actions in order:
  1. Colours card.
  2. Ranked list.
  3. Slider.
  4. Second pick, then back to the top pick.
  5. + Jewellery.
  6. + Lip colour.
  7. Look card.
  8. "Why colour matching matters".

  One continuous take is fine; trim it in the edit.
- **Clip B (scene 10):** open https://complete-look.vercel.app/look/kavya and scroll to the ranked list.
- **Clip C (scene 12):** open https://complete-look.vercel.app/try, take or choose a photo, tap **Build my complete look**, and wait until all 5 steps are done. Speed it up in the edit.

**💻 Desktop (screen recording, browser about 1280 px wide): scenes 11 and 14.** The X-ray JSON and the URL are easier to read on a wide screen.
- **Clip D (scene 11):** open https://complete-look.vercel.app/look/ananya, scroll to the X-ray, open one **What we sent**, and end on the units/time row. Zoom in during the edit if the text is small.
- **Clip E (scene 14):** the landing page with the address bar visible. Hold for 10 s as the end card.

Record the voice-over separately (a phone voice memo in a quiet room is fine), then lay the clips over it.

## Recording notes

- **Scene 12 (live run):** do one dry run with your selfie first. The recording run then hits our cache (same photo, about 0 units, fast). If it shows "Demo limit reached", that's the per-visitor cap of 30 units; raise `YOUCAM_SESSION_UNIT_CAP` to 90 in Vercel for the recording day and set it back after. Don't use your real photo if you'd rather not show your face; a sample photo from the repo works too.
- Scenes 1 and 3–11 are replays: no spinners and no units, so retake them as often as you like.
- Don't show any third-party logos or brand names. The video must name each YouCam API, as scenes 3, 5, 6, 7 and 11 do.
- Upload unlisted first, watch it on the phone, check the captions, then make it public and put the link in the README (`VIDEO_URL`) and on Devpost.
